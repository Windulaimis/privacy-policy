Privacy Policy for VolleyGuide

Last updated: October 30, 2026

VolleyGuide (“the app”) is a volleyball learning, training and coaching app built by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). The app is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

In short: everything you enter in VolleyGuide is stored only on your own device. We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts on the internet, and we do not use advertising, analytics or tracking. Information only leaves your device when you search for a club or when you choose to share something.

1. Information You Enter in the App

To use VolleyGuide you create an account as a Player or a Coach. The information you enter stays on your device and is never sent to us. This includes:

  • Account details: name, nickname, email address, country, and your team or club (optional). Your email is only used as your sign-in name on this device.
  • Password: never stored as plain text. It is turned into a salted, one-way hash and kept in your device’s encrypted secure storage.
  • Pictures you choose: a profile picture and a team emblem, if you add them.
  • Player information: position, experience level, XP, streaks, completed lessons and trainings, logged games and game statistics, notes, and your friends list.
  • Coach information: teams and rosters (player names, shirt numbers and positions), game plans, court plays, team trainings, hand signals, and live match statistics.

2. Information That Leaves Your Device

VolleyGuide only sends information off your device in these cases:

  • Club search: when you search for your club, the country and the club name you type are sent to TheSportsDB (www.thesportsdb.com), a public sports database, to look up matching clubs. No other information is sent.
  • Sharing you start yourself: when you tap a share button, the app prepares a message or link and passes it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, email or messages). This includes team invites, friend requests, game confirmations, training plans, game plans, hand signals, match reports and Excel (CSV) exports. These contain the information shown on screen, such as names, rosters, statistics or plans. Links contain this information inside them, so only share them with people you trust. Once shared, that information is handled by the app you used and by the people who receive it.

We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

3. Permissions

  • Photos: used only when you choose a profile picture or team emblem. Only the picture you select is copied into the app’s private storage.
  • Face ID / fingerprint (optional): if you turn on biometric sign-in, your device checks your face or fingerprint. The app never receives or stores your biometric data. It is only told whether the check succeeded.
  • Internet: used for the club search and for opening links shared with you.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

4. Data Security

Your data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, and passwords are kept as salted hashes in encrypted secure storage. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock, as anyone who can unlock your device may be able to open the app.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual games, teams, game plans, trainings, signs and friends inside the app.
  • Delete everything, including your account, by uninstalling the app, or on Android via Settings → Apps → VolleyGuide → Storage → Clear storage.

Because we do not store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Information you have already shared with other people cannot be removed by us.

6. Children’s Privacy

VolleyGuide can be used by players of all ages, including youth teams. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because all information stays on the device. Coaches who add players under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country) to a roster should get permission from a parent or guardian, and should remove a player’s information at a parent’s request. Parents who have questions can contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

Future versions of VolleyGuide may add online features, such as signing in with Google or syncing teams between devices. If that happens, we will update this policy before those features are released and change the “Last updated” date above.
